**A3 — Every other write route is unauthenticated and cross-origin.** `brain/server.py` L266–268 (`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` on all of `/dash/api/`), L225–257 (`/volume`, `/hear`, `/game`, `/say`, `/head`, `/settings`, `/content`, `/rig` with no origin check); `brain/dashboard.py` L46–59 (no Origin or Content-Type check, so a `text/plain` form post works). `rig()` is not gated to test mode (`brain/dash.py` L510–518): a page the operator has open can fix who the Mafia is for the next game. ## B. Hearing: what the ears make of a sentence

**B1 — "reset", "start over" and "stop playing" inside any sentence end the game, at every listen, including the two-minute discussion.** `brain/games/common.py` L97–102 wraps every listen in `hear()`, which raises `GameStopped` on `ears.wants_to_stop`; `wants_to_stop` (`brain/ears.py` L173–176) is a substring test over the joined tokens and `wants_to_reset` (L179–184) a regex with no negation. Traces: a player saying "i think we should start over with the accusations" during the day → "Okay, starting over." and the welcome; the psychic's clue "restart" ends Wavelength; "don't reset" resets; "stop playing dumb, player two" stops. D27/D36 say the group may restart *by saying so*; D38 names the three phrases. → **D87** (a stop or a reset is a short utterance that is mostly the command: at most five words, no negation, said on its own, not inside a sentence; during a vote or a whisper the host asks once more before acting on it) → `abdd/3-src`: `wants_to_stop`/`wants_to_reset` anchored to short utterances without "don't"/"not", and the discussion's chunks no longer stop the game on a substring.

**B2 — "restart the round" is a full reset to the welcome.** D27/D36 (ratified `[V]`) say the group may restart a *round*; `_STOP` (`ears.py` L42–45) and `_RESET` (L179) read it as a reset and no code path restarts a round. → **D88** (a round restart in Mafia starts the current night again with the same roles and no deaths undone; "reset" alone goes to the welcome) → `abdd/3-src`: `GameStopped(round=True)` handled in `mafia.run`.

**B3 — A disagreement is heard as a go-ahead and the game starts on the host's count.** D6: the start waits until the spoken number and the host's count agree. `gather()` (`common.py` L174–181) breaks on `ears.agrees()` when the reply has no number; `_AGREE` (`ears.py` L47–48) is a bare-word list with "right", "start", "fine", "okay", "let's go" and no negation: "no that's not right" → the game starts on five. The go-ahead itself ("okay" accepts the host's count), the give-up after three tries (`Options.count_tries`, `common.py` L44, L170–173; `lines.COUNT_GIVE_UP`), silence as agreement (L164–166) and "I see N. Is someone just watching?" (`lines.py` L77–79, whose docstring says "not ruled") are all unruled; `test/py/test_games_common.py` L29–37 asserts the give-up *as* D6. → **D89** (a plain go-ahead with no "no" or "not" in it accepts the host's count; a disagreement with no number gets a recount; after three disagreements the host says it will try again when everyone is ready and no game starts) and **D90** (a spoken count below the host's gets "I see N. Is someone just watching?" and a recount, since someone may be out of the camera's view) → `abdd/3-src`: `agrees()` rejects negated replies; the recount path per D90.

**B4 — Soundalikes and pointers read as counts and votes.** `bare_number` (`ears.py` L144–150) takes "you two" → 2, "me too" → 2, "one sec" → 1, "sex" → 6, "i ate" → 8 as a spoken count after the count line (a recount or a give-up follows); `seat_said` via `_one_number` (L93–109) reads "me too" → 2, "everyone except player four" → 4, "anyone but four" → 4, "players three and four" → 3, "player four, no wait, two" → 4 (the correction lost). Two voters saying "me too" after a first vote for two produce a 2-2-1 tie. `test/py/test_ears.py` L50–76 asserts the soundalike readings as desired. → **D91** (a vote names exactly one living seat; a vote with two seats, a negation or a correction is asked for again; soundalikes count only right after "player", "number" or "seat") → `abdd/3-src`: `_one_number` rejects negation and multi-seat utterances, takes the last number after a correction word, soundalikes only after a pointer.

**B5 — An early vote on a substring skips the timed day.** `wants_to_vote` (`ears.py` L276–279): "i'm not ready to vote", "we're ready to hear from player two", "don't vote now" all vote early; D18's "One minute left." is then never said. The early vote itself is unruled. → **D92** (the group may vote early by saying so in a short plain sentence; the halfway call is still said if it has not been; a negated or longer sentence is talk) → `abdd/3-src`: anchored phrase match.

**B6 — Pause phrases.** "hold on" does not pause (`ears.py` L187 `_PAUSE` is pause / pause the game / hold the game) though DASHBOARD-PLAN P1 says it does, and in Hot Seat "hold on" is judged as an answer and counts while "wait" puts the player out (`brain/games/hotseat.py` L103–124; `hotseat_words.py` L133–137). **B7 — A game's name fires inside ordinary sentences.** `game_named` (`ears.py` L111–122): "that was a hot seat" → Hot Seat; "the word game" → Wordle; "i have twenty questions about this" → Twenty Questions; "put it in poster form" → Imposter; and the welcome starts that game at once (`brain/games/welcome.py` L130–133). → **D94** (a game starts on its name said as a request or as the whole utterance, "let's play X", "X", "X please", not on the name inside other talk; a doubtful hearing gets "Mafia? **B8 — Wavelength's guess.** `number_said` (`ears.py` L160–164) returns None for "seven out of ten", "eight or nine, let's say eight", "nine? no, eight"; a one-word soundalike scores ("sex" → 6, "ate" → 8, "tin" → 10); the first parsable chunk scores while the group is still talking ("it's definitely hot, like an eight" → 8).
